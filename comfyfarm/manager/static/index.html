From ShotGrid

A ShotGrid Version opens the Submit form with it as the input; a Playlist, or several Versions, open Batch Submit with a row each. Nothing is sent until you submit.

The ComfyFarm button

Drag this onto your browser's bookmarks bar (once): ComfyFarm

Then on a ShotGrid page with a Version or a Playlist open (its own page, its overlay, the Media Center), click it. It reads the page's address, so on a page that lists many, open the Version first, or drag it here instead. It opens in a tab of its own, reused the next time.

Drag and drop

Drag a Version's or a Playlist's name from ShotGrid onto this page (onto its tab first, if they share a window). Dropped on a field, it's pasted there as its link.

Right-click in ShotGrid

Send to ComfyFarm in a Version's or a Playlist's right-click menu, where it's been set up for your show (README: ShotGrid right-click). Several Versions selected go to Batch Submit together.
